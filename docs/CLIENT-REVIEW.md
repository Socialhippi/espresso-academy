# Espresso Academy India: draft website for review

**Review URL: https://espresso-academy-india.vercel.app**

Please open it **on your phone**, not a laptop. Around two thirds of the people who will use this
site are on a phone, and it is designed for that first.

## Before you start, two things to know

**Everything marked TBC is waiting on you, not broken.** No fee, batch date, duration or opening
hour has been published anywhere we could check, so rather than print a plausible number the site
shows a grey "TBC" pill. Send us the real values and they appear everywhere at once. The full list
is in `docs/STATUS.md`.

**No photographs yet.** Every image slot shows a branded grey placeholder with the slot name on it,
for example "Photo: hero". Drop the shoot files into the folder with the names in
`docs/images-manifest.md` and the placeholders disappear. Nothing on this site is an AI-generated
photograph and nothing is stock.

## The ten-minute review

Work through these in order and note anything you would say differently. One numbered list back to
us is much easier to act on than several messages.

1. **Home.** Does the first screen tell you what the academy is and where, before you scroll? Then
   tap "See the courses".
2. **Courses.** Tap the "Foundation" chip. Do the cards make sense? Note anything you would word
   differently.
3. **Open one course.** Read "Who this is for" and "Who this is not for". Are they true? These are
   the two blocks that stop the wrong person enrolling, so they matter more than they look.
4. **Scroll to the fees and batches.** Everything marked TBC is waiting for your numbers. Send them
   and they appear.
5. **Tap "Reserve a seat".** Fill the form in with your own number and submit it. Did you get the
   WhatsApp handoff?
6. **Tap the WhatsApp button in the bottom bar.** Is the pre-filled message right? Is it the right
   number? We are currently using +91 94481 06100 for both calls and WhatsApp because we could not
   confirm which number is on WhatsApp.
7. **Trainers.** Are the credentials correct and complete? Anything missing or out of date? Roles
   and a line in each trainer's own words are both still blank, deliberately.
8. **Certifications.** Read the IBC page. Is every sentence something you would say to a student?
9. **About.** Is the Florence story right? Is "Official Partner of Espresso Academy, Florence" the
   wording your agreement allows? We have deliberately not claimed anything about India as a whole,
   because Espresso Academy also lists a partner in New Delhi.
10. **Contact.** Is the address exact? Does "Open in Google Maps" land on the right building? The
    plot number needs confirming: your site says 72, the brochure says 9.

## What we need from you to finish

The five that unblock the most:

1. The course list with the fee incl. GST, duration, level and next batch date for each.
2. The WhatsApp number, a public email address and your opening hours.
3. Written confirmation of the "Official Partner of Espresso Academy, Florence" wording, and where
   you stand on SCA campus and trainer AST status.
4. The logo as an SVG. We only have raster renders, and the stacked lockup's wordmark is too small
   to read at header size, so the header currently pairs the mark with the academy name set in
   type. That needs your sign-off or a horizontal lockup.
5. Any student stories you have written permission to publish. The stories section stays empty
   until then; we will not write one.

## One thing to decide

Two claims from the brochure are on the site nowhere, because we could not verify them: "17
branches worldwide" and the Berry Co partnership. Tell us which brochure claims are current and we
will add the ones you can stand behind.

## How to send changes

Reply with **one numbered list**, from one decision-maker, with the page name and the change. For
anything visual, an example website or a photograph is worth far more than a description. We will
do one revision round, then approval.
