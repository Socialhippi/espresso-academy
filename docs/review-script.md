# Client review script (10 minutes, on your phone)

Open the preview link on your phone, not a laptop. Most students will see it this way.

1. Home: does the first screen tell you what the academy is and where, before you scroll? Tap "See courses and dates".
2. Courses: tap "Foundation". Do the cards make sense? Note anything you would say differently.
3. Open one course. Read "Who this is for" and "Who this is not for". Are they true?
4. Scroll to fees and batches. Everything marked TBC is waiting for your numbers; send them and they appear.
5. Tap "Reserve a seat". Fill the form with your own number and submit. Did you get the WhatsApp handoff?
6. Tap the WhatsApp button in the bottom bar. Is the pre-filled message right? Is it the right number?
7. Trainers: are the credentials correct and complete? Anything missing or outdated?
8. Certifications: read the IBC page. Is every sentence something you would say to a student?
9. About: is the Florence story right? Is "Official Partner of Espresso Academy, Florence" the wording your agreement allows?
10. Contact: is the address exact? Does "Open in Google Maps" land on the right building?

Reply with one list, numbered, with the page name and the change. Anything that is "make it look more X" please describe with an example website or a photo. We will do one revision round, then approval.
